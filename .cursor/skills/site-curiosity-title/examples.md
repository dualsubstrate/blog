# Curiosity title — examples

Reference for **`.cursor/skills/site-curiosity-title/SKILL.md`**. Legitimate tension only.

## Pass (gap + payoff)

| Title | Gap | Payoff lives in |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| Why does every group organize into a pyramid? | Flat ideals keep losing; groups tier anyway | Claim: layers, rank, attractor; body: families → VP meeting |
| Your org chart mirrors the pyramid already in your head | Org feels imposed; brain pre-stacked | Same post (earlier title variant); needs Claim to name brain layers |
| Why does your feed reward noise over skill? | Scrolling feels neutral; ranking is biased | Claim about incentive / ranking (hypothetical social-protocols pick) |

## Fail (bait or fog)

| Title | Why fail |
|-------|----------|
| The hidden truth about hierarchy | Mystery box; no concrete image |
| Why do systems fail? | Too broad; no answer scoped in one post |
| Brains do not map complexity flat | Jargon; negation without an image |
| …route attention through control systems that sit above local detail | Claim fog; dual-meaning “systems,” no picture |
| …route attention upward, so local noise gets summarized before it reaches a decision | Claim pass; direction + outcome |
| You won't believe how groups organize | Fake urgency; no specific gap |

## Pattern A variations (same post)

| Candidate | Note |
|-----------|------|
| Why does every group organize into a pyramid? | **Recommended** — clearest cold-read |
| Why does every group redraw the pyramid? | Strong "again" metaphor; slightly less literal |
| Why does your flat org chart turn into a pyramid? | More corporate-reader specific |
| Why do humans keep building pyramids? | Broader; matches closing line |

## Claim pairing (claims type)

**Title (question):** Why does every group organize into a pyramid?

**Claim (answer):** Brains compress the world into layers, track rank, … Hierarchy is an attractor, not destiny. …

Do **not** make the Claim only: "Why do groups become pyramids?" without the mechanism.
