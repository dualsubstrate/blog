Claim

Eight stops on one cube, twenty hop kinds, and a separate roll plus face-flip bit. The interactive view below is the same hops canvas we use in Cursor, published for the blog.

Thoughts

This page is the public copy of the DSS hops canvas: Ring and M on the two tetrahedra, twelve cube rim edges, Roll, and FF. Drag the cube, pick a hop pill, and open the Resolution tab for the count equations.

Stops, rolls, and FF

The Stops × rolls column is eight landing points times twenty-four cube rolls. With FF multiplies by two for face flip on the face the hop already sits on. That is separate from the twenty walk kinds in the hop table.

Grounding

Stops are corners 0–7. Roll is 6 faces down × 4 turns (24). Face flip (FF) is a binary on the current face. Resolution on the exhibit: 8stp * 6fce * 4trn * 2ff = 384. Walk kinds are 4 Ring + 4 M + 12 cube edges; the store is the hop product along a path, not stops × kinds.
