About

This blog documents the Dual Substrate System: governed memory, lineage, and evaluation for agents that must stay accountable to people and policy.

It is separate from the marketing site but aligned with it. Essays and claims here explain mechanisms; the product lives at dualsubstrate.com.

What you will find

  • Platform: principals, grants, ledgers, and surfaces.
  • Lineage: coordinates, retrieval, grounding, and memory policy.
  • Proof: benchmarks, DSS-EVAL, and known limits.
  • Notes: shipping updates and operator-facing detail.
